Common questions

Not sure what you need? Start here, or message us and we'll help.

My AC runs but the room won't cool.

Several things can cause this, including dirty filters, low refrigerant or a failing fan or compressor. It takes an inspection to know which. Book a repair and tell us what you see.

Water is dripping from the indoor unit.

This is often a blocked drain line or a clogged filter. Switch the unit off so the water doesn't damage walls or floors, then book a visit.

The unit is noisy or smells bad.

Unusual noises can point to loose or worn parts. Smells often come from dirty filters or coils. A maintenance visit is a good first step for smells; noises may need a repair.

How often should I service my AC?

Once a year is the common recommendation. A check before summer helps your system cope when Cairo's heat peaks. Systems that run all day, like those in shops and offices, may need visits more often.

Can you get a spare part for my unit?

Spare parts are one of our services. Send us the brand, the model and a photo of the label on the unit, and we'll check what we can supply.

What should I have ready when I book?

Your name, phone number, address, the type of unit and a short description of the problem. A photo of the unit's label helps for repairs and parts.
